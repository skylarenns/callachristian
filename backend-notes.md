# Backend concept note

Frontend-only for now. Do not implement routing until the public number, responder workflow, and service boundaries are settled.

- Visitors contact one shared public number.
- Visitors need no account or app.
- The two responders use an app or dashboard to receive and answer conversations.
- Incoming calls and messages route to available responders.
- The initial public expectation is a reply within 24 hours.
- Phone/SMS provider and responder app remain undecided.
- Future planning must cover privacy, abuse prevention, emergency boundaries, message ownership, and responder availability.
